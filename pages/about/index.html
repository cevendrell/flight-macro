About

Reading the world
through flight data.

OvrHead is a research project that treats global flight-route volume as an economic instrument. Airlines respond to demand faster than official statistics can capture it — a route that adds frequency in July is telling you something about consumer confidence, cross-border capital, or supply-chain reshaping before it shows up in a GDP release.

What this is

A live map of macro signals inferred from route-level flight data. Each signal pairs a quantitative change (frequency, seat capacity, booking curves) with a qualitative reading — a hypothesis about the macroeconomic driver behind it.

What this is not

Not a flight tracker. Not a booking tool. Not a forecast. The readings are hypotheses, calibrated to available data, and every signal ships with a confidence level.
